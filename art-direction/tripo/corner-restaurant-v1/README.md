# CIVRUM 转角餐馆 · Tripo 三视图

首层折角入口连接两面临街橱窗。二层为浅色石材和内退露台。招牌留白，便于后续在 Blender 中换字。正面与背面的外轮廓宽度约有 9% 的出图偏差，建筑落位时以同一底面尺寸为准。

多视图上传使用 [正面](01-front.png)、[右侧面](02-right.png) 和 [背面](03-back.png)。面朝正面时取建筑右侧，侧面图的左端为临街正面。三张独立 PNG 均为 940 × 724，导出时保留原图像素和竖直位置。[三视图总图](three-views.png) 用于核对视角。

### 校正

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit, architectural turnaround for Tripo. The attached image is the edit target. Preserve one exact original two-floor CIVRUM corner restaurant design: the same cream limestone upper floor, graphite lower facade, dark teal wooden metal window frames, blank brass-lined signage fascia, principal upper recessed terraced balcony, and dark glazing. Keep the sheet with exactly three orthographic elevations horizontally (front, right side, back), on the same plain light gray stage without any text.
Make ONLY these cross-view corrections, precise enough for mesh generation:
1. In the middle RIGHT elevation, shorten the right street facade horizontally about 20 percent; in real measurements the ground structure is 12 meters across front or back and 9.5 meters along right side. Front and back width and building height remain the same. The right-side 45-degree corner entrance must remain at the LEFT edge of the middle view. Keep both wide side shop window groups and both upper right side windows; narrow the gaps/inactive limestone stretches to fit.
2. Remove the projecting cream hanging sign and its exterior pole or bracket on the left of both visible elevations. Keep the embedded wide graphite fascia blank and unchanged.
3. All edges of intentionally horizontal physical surfaces (cornice lines, storefront canopy, plinth / zero plane) must read as perfectly horizontal in an elevation, also across the front-right chamfer. Correct any tilted lines at the corners. Keep the original 45-degree chamfer (front visible at right, right side visible at left, hidden at rear), fixtures, mullions, neutral studio lights and materials. Strict ground-level camera angles: 0, 90, 180 degrees, same pixels/metre, same shared roof top and base lines. Full silhouettes with wide margins and more than 5% clear blank space between panels. Tall rectangular windows fully vertical. Ultra-wide 3:1 high resolution. No perspective, no roof top visible, no signage text or props.
```
